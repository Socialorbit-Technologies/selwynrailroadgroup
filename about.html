About us

Railroaders serving railroads

Selwyn Rail Road operates short line freight service and provides the track, mechanical, signal, logistics and emergency services that keep railroads and rail-served businesses running.

Who we are

Many crews, one company

We are made up of several specialist teams: railroad operations, track construction and maintenance, mechanical repair, signaling, material supply and emergency response. Each team is strong on its own. Together they give customers one partner for the whole job.

That matters when work crosses trades. A new industrial spur needs track, signals and a plan for first shipments. A derailment needs heavy equipment, track repair and car repair at the same time. We coordinate it all under one name and one phone number.

Our vision

Be the best service provider to the railroad and the industries it serves.

Our mission is to get there through dedicated employees, a total commitment to customer satisfaction, efficient operations, and a workplace that keeps getting safer and more rewarding.

Safety

Nothing is so urgent that it can't be done safely

Working on or near the track is unforgiving. Our safety team visits every location with training, audits and a sharp eye, and every employee commits to safe work before the first job.

Our safety principles
  • Our goal is zero injuries.
  • Working safely is a condition of employment.
  • Safety training never stops.
  • Every family is counting on us to come home safe.
  • Safe work is good business.

Every employee commits to

  1. Work safelyPerform every task in a safe manner.
  2. Wear the right PPEProper personal protective equipment at all times.
  3. Hold job briefingsAt the start of work and whenever conditions change.
  4. Know the hazardsIdentify and report the hazards of each job.
  5. Follow the rulesComply with every safety guideline and program.
  6. Report right awayReport all incidents and near misses immediately.

Public safety

Stay off the tracks

Trains cannot stop quickly and they overhang the rails. Please keep these rules in mind near any railroad.

  • Trespassing on railroad property is illegal
  • Cross only at marked crossings
  • Always expect a train
  • Stand well back from the track
  • Never try to beat a train
