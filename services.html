Rail operations

Railroad shipping & logistics

We operate short line freight service and connect your shipments to the Class I network through our interchange partners.

Our customer service team helps with rates, car orders, tracing and billing so your freight keeps moving.

  • Carload service
  • Track and railcar leasing
  • Load adjustments
  • Warehouse space and distribution
  • Shipment tracking and tracing support

Industrial switching

We place crews and locomotives inside your facility to build, break down and spot cars where you need them.

Our switching teams can also maintain your yard tracks and your locomotives, so plant rail runs as one operation.

  • Full-service in-plant switching
  • Railcar loading and unloading
  • Rail yard and track maintenance
  • Locomotive maintenance
  • Inventory and outbound loading support

Railcar storage & cleaning

Store loaded or empty cars on our lines for a few days or a few years, with interchange access when it is time to release them.

Our teams remove dunnage, debris and residual product so each car is ready for its next load.

  • Short-term and long-term storage
  • Tank, box, hopper, flat and covered cars
  • Unit train interchange
  • Light repairs at storage locations
  • On-site or yard-based railcar cleaning

Transloading & logistics

Transloading

Transloading moves your product between trucks and railcars, so you can use rail for the long haul even without a rail siding at your door.

We match the equipment to the commodity and offer full-service or self-serve set-ups at our transload sites.

  • Lumber, metals and coils
  • Food-grade bulk products
  • Plastics and resins
  • Energy liquids and gases
  • Chemicals and hazardous materials
  • Aggregates, minerals, paper and pulp

Rail-served distribution centers

Receive bulk shipments by rail, store them securely under roof, and deliver to your customers as they need product.

Just-in-time inventory management shortens your cycle times and keeps your plant supplied.

  • Secure indoor and fenced outdoor storage
  • Overhead crane and heavy forklift handling
  • Truck and rail scales
  • Material handling and repackaging
  • Just-in-time delivery

Material supply & inventory management

We source, stock and deliver track and signal material, so your crews have what they need where they need it.

Material can be kitted to your specifications and delivered by hi-rail truck right to the point of installation.

  • 24/7 material service
  • Hi-rail delivery and final distribution
  • Material kitted to specifications
  • Track panel construction and delivery
  • Scrap reclamation

Track services

Railroad track construction

From a new industrial spur to a mainline siding, our crews handle planning, roadbed preparation, track laying and final inspection.

We deliver turnkey projects and work around your operating schedule.

  • New track, sidings and spurs
  • Roadbed and site preparation
  • Switch and turnout installation
  • Grade crossing installation
  • Continuous welded rail and thermite welding

Railroad track maintenance

Scheduled maintenance keeps track in service and prevents costly emergency repairs.

We build maintenance plans around your facility and respond fast when something fails.

  • Tie and rail replacement
  • Surfacing and ballast renewal
  • Drainage and culvert work
  • Switch and turnout cleaning
  • Annual maintenance programs

Maintenance of way

Our maintenance-of-way crews keep the right-of-way clear, drained and safe for traffic.

Specialized hi-rail equipment lets us work efficiently with fewer track outages.

  • Right-of-way clearing and brush cutting
  • Track undercutting
  • Joint replacement
  • Bridge and tunnel clearance work
  • Hi-rail excavators, dumps and vac trucks

Railroad track inspection

Our inspectors check mainline, yard and industrial track to FRA and industry standards and find defects before they cause service problems.

You receive clear reports your maintenance team can act on.

  • Scheduled track inspections
  • Post-storm and heavy-use assessments
  • Gauge, geometry and wear checks
  • Defect reporting and repair planning
  • Compliance documentation

Keep cars and locomotives in service.

Mechanical repair

Railcar repair

AAR-certified technicians repair railcars in our shops or at your site with mobile crews.

Fast turnaround reduces out-of-service time and protects the life of your fleet.

  • Brake and wheelset replacement
  • Structural welding
  • Warranty work
  • Emergency repairs
  • Mobile and in-shop service

Locomotive repair

We maintain locomotives for railroads, transload operations and industrial plants, from scheduled inspections to heavy repairs.

Our mobile crews can diagnose and repair units on site when moving them to a shop is not practical.

  • 92-day preventative maintenance
  • FRA inspection preparation
  • Mechanical and electrical repairs
  • Wheel, truck and traction motor change-outs
  • Event recorder, camera and A/C installs
  • Load testing

Emergency response

24/7 emergency response

When a derailment stops traffic, our crews and heavy equipment respond around the clock to clear the line and get trains running again.

Call +1 (407) 760-8000 at any hour to reach dispatch.

  • 24/7 emergency dispatch
  • Re-railing locomotives and cars
  • Side boom and heavy equipment crews
  • Post-derailment clean-up
  • Hazmat removal support
  • Track repair after an incident

Natural disaster response

Hurricanes, floods and washouts can take out miles of track at once. Our storm response teams combine crews and equipment from every part of the company to rebuild quickly.

We can set up temporary base camps so crews can work until the line is open.

  • Track reconstruction
  • Rock and riprap hauling
  • Track materials delivered on site
  • Temporary base camp infrastructure
  • Clean-up and re-railing
  • Aerial damage assessment
